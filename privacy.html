Privacy policy

Page updated: 6 October 2026

This page describes the features included in the Obskyra personal homepage.

Display preferences

The homepage saves your chosen background and screensaver in your browser's local storage under obskyra-desktop. The homepage code reads these settings on later visits; it does not send them to a server. They remain until you clear this site's browser data. If storage is unavailable, the controls still work for the current visit.

Page features

The homepage code does not set cookies and does not include an analytics script, advertising tracker, account system or contact form. Its inactivity timer and animations run in your browser. The code does not save your mouse movements or keystrokes.

Hosting

obskyra.stream is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you visit, Cloudflare processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser details and the page you requested. This site does not add its own logging or analytics on top of that. Cloudflare explains how it handles this information in its privacy policy.

Other websites and contact

Twitch, YouTube, TikTok and Discord are external services. Following a link takes you to that service, where its own privacy practices apply.

For questions about this homepage, use the Discord community. Community messages may be visible to other members; do not post sensitive personal information publicly.
